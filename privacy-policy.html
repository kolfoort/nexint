Enquiries and business contacts

When you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, company, business contact details and the contents of your enquiry. We use these to respond and manage prospective or existing business relationships. Email and WhatsApp links open the relevant external service; its own privacy terms also apply.

Client accounts and company profiles

We process account identifiers, email addresses, authentication records, access rights, company details, contact details and the information you submit in your company profile. These records allow us to invite clients, provide secure access, review profiles and administer business relationships. Required fields are identified in the portal; without the necessary information, we may be unable to provide access or process your request.

Requests for portal access

If you request access, we process the company and contact information you provide, your message, submission time and review status to assess and respond to the request. Access is subject to review or invitation; submitting a request does not itself grant access to documents. Relevant information may be copied into your company profile when an account is created.

Documents, agreements and signatures

When documents are shared or uploaded, we process the files and their contents, document titles, messages, recipient assignments, submission times and review status. Agreements may also contain signatory names, roles, signature images, signing dates and related approval records. For agreements that require approval and countersignature, we also process the relevant approval records, timestamps and document hashes used to identify the approved version.

Documents are stored in private portal storage and made available according to account permissions. Authorized Nexora International administrators manage client files and documents. Access is limited by the portal’s authorization rules. A recipient may keep a downloaded copy; withdrawing portal access does not remove copies already downloaded.

Product and chartering requests

Our Performance Fluids, Crude Oils and Marine Chartering forms submit your requirements to the Nexora platform. We store company and contact details, the request reference, product or vessel requirements, quantities, delivery or voyage information, messages, submission times and status updates. Requests are linked to a client profile; new clients receive an account activation or invitation message, while existing clients use their authenticated account. We send a confirmation and relevant status notifications by email and notify the Nexora team internally.

We process this information to respond to your request, take pre-contractual steps where applicable and manage business enquiries. For abuse prevention, the request service applies submission limits and uses a keyed pseudonymous identifier derived from connection information. Request and delivery records are retained while needed to handle the enquiry, maintain the business relationship or meet applicable legal obligations or claims.

Service emails

We use your email address for invitations, password recovery and document or workflow notifications. Email records can include delivery status and technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot these messages. These are service communications; acknowledging this notice does not subscribe you to marketing.

Why we process this information

We rely on contractual necessity or steps taken at your request before a contract where applicable; legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries, secure document exchange, access control and preventing misuse; and legal obligations where these apply. We balance legitimate interests against your rights. If we rely on consent for a separate purpose, we explain that purpose and you can withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Service providers and sharing

Supabase provides portal authentication, database services and private document storage. TransIP provides email services. GitHub Pages hosts the public website and may process connection data, including IP addresses, in technical logs. The portal loads its authentication library from esm.sh, which can receive technical connection information when the library is downloaded. Authorized personnel and service providers receive information needed for their tasks; professional advisers or authorities may receive relevant records where necessary for a transaction, legal obligation or legal claim. We do not sell personal data.

Nexora International and VIP Capital use separate portal databases and account administration. A Nexora account does not grant access to the VIP Capital portal.

Browser storage and cookies

The portal uses sessionStorage to retain the authentication session in the current browser tab. This is necessary to keep you signed in and authorize portal requests. Signing out clears the local session. Closing the tab normally ends this storage, although browser session restoration can affect this. The website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Technical information may also be processed by hosting and authentication providers to deliver and protect their services.

Retention

We retain enquiries while needed to respond and manage any resulting relationship. Account and profile records are retained while needed to administer access and the relevant business relationship. Documents, signatures and approval records are retained for the relevant transaction, applicable legal obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Disabling an account or changing a review status does not automatically erase its records. Contact us for the retention criteria applicable to your file or to request closure or deletion. Provider logs and backups may follow separate retention and deletion cycles.

International processing and security

Our service providers may process data outside the Netherlands or the European Economic Area. Where a transfer requires safeguards, applicable mechanisms may include an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses; contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data. Access controls, private storage and authenticated sessions help protect portal information. Do not upload information that is unnecessary for your business request.

Your rights

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact info@nexint.eu. We may need proportionate information to verify your identity. We normally respond within one month; an extension may apply for complex or numerous requests, and we will inform you where required. You may complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority.

Changes

We update this notice when our website, portal or data practices change. The revision date appears above.
