Your trusted

Energy, feedstocks & industrial solutions.

Nexora is a Dutch family-owned trading and supply company specializing in energy products, industrial chemicals, feedstocks, fertilizers and performance fluids.

We connect serious buyers with reliable supply sources through direct channels, transparent communication and efficient execution. Depending on the transaction, Nexora also acts as principal, buying and selling in its own name and taking title to the goods. Our role is made clear from the outset: all parties know before proceeding whether Nexora is acting as principal or facilitating the transaction. My focus is straightforward: delivering competitive solutions that work.

Direct. Reliable. Efficient.

We work with verified suppliers and trusted logistics partners to handle transactions professionally, from inquiry to delivery.
